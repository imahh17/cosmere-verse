# Home

Mode: Persuade. Audience: a reader new to the Cosmere. Job: understand that the Cosmere is one universe of standalone sagas, travel its worlds, and leave with a first book. Action: start the journey (scroll through the worlds) and reach the reading route. Proof/content: real titles, original publication years, worlds and magic systems; no spoilers, no invented claims. Constraints: unofficial fan site, no official art; reduced motion and no-JS must keep every word readable.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard, played straight: deep space and rendered planets, the visit as a camera journey. Refuses the flat starfield with a row of planet cards: the planets are the navigation and the page travels to them.
OWN-WORLD: Blue-black space with a real-time WebGL starfield; procedurally shaded planets with atmospheric rims, one colour world per planet that also tints that section's type and rules. Huge condensed planet names, a hyperlegible sans for reading, hairline leader rules tying books to their planet.
STORY: The visitor learns the Cosmere is many worlds, one universe; sees each world's premise and books in order; ends on a four-step reading route and picks a first book.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Scadrial fills the lower right as a lit sphere with the next worlds receding behind it; top left a 6rem headline, one-sentence lead, primary "Empieza el viaje" and secondary "Ruta de lectura"; header with wordmark and two links.
FORM: Category canon (standing exit, user-chosen), code-led, seed: 35a02853ff4f53d0. Signature interaction: scroll-scrubbed, damped camera flight from planet to planet; each stop pins the planet beside its text.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
